Pickleball
Rules & Guidelines

1. General Rules

  • The tournament will be conducted according to the latest USA Pickleball rules.
  • All players are expected to demonstrate good sportsmanship and fair play.
  • The Core Committee's decision (based on the Pickleball Subcommittee) will be final in case of any disputes.

2. Match Format

  • Up to the semi-finals: one game of 21 points. Point no. 21 will be the golden point, which decides the winner.
  • Up to the semi-finals: teams will switch sides when either team reaches 11 points.
  • Semi-finals and finals: best of three games of 11 points. The winner of a game is the team that reaches 11 points first. Point no. 11 will be the golden point.
  • Semi-finals and finals: teams will switch sides in the third game when either team reaches 6 points.
  • The team that wins the toss (e.g. Team A) can choose to serve or choose a side. If Team A chooses to serve, the opposing team can choose their preferred side.
  • One time-out is allowed per match for each team.

3. Scoring

  • Rally point scoring will be used.

4. Serving Rules

  • The serve must be made diagonally into the opponent's service court.
  • Only one serve attempt is allowed.
  • At least one foot must remain behind the baseline during the serve.

5. Two-Bounce Rule

  • The receiving team must allow the serve to bounce before returning it.
  • The serving team must also allow the return of serve to bounce before hitting it.
  • After these two bounces, volleys are allowed while players are outside the Kitchen (Non-Volley Zone).

6. Kitchen (Non-Volley Zone) Rules

  • Players may not volley the ball while standing in the Kitchen.
  • A player cannot step on the Kitchen line during a volley.
  • Momentum carrying a player into the Kitchen after a volley is also a fault.

7. Time Regulations

  • Players must report to the court at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.
  • Any team that fails to report within 10 minutes of the called match time may forfeit the match, and a walkover will be awarded to the opposing team.

8. Equipment

  • Non-marking court shoes are mandatory.
  • USPA-approved pickleball paddles must be used.

9. Conduct

  • Unsportsmanlike behaviour, abusive language or misconduct may result in a warning or disqualification.

10. Injury & Safety

  • Players participate at their own risk.
  • Tournament organisers are not responsible for injuries sustained during play.

11. Ball Hitting the Roof

  • If the ball touches the roof or any overhead structure during play, it shall be considered a fault.
  • The point will be awarded to the team opposing the player/team that caused the ball to hit the roof.

Flower Arrangement
Rules & Guidelines

Team Composition

  • Each association may participate with only one team, consisting of 3 members.
  • There is no age restriction.

Style & Materials

  • The arrangement must be freestyle, using fresh flowers and fresh leaves.
  • Artificial accessories, fillers and props may be used sparingly.

Preparation

  • All cutting of flowers and leaves must be done at the venue.
  • Participants may soak the Oasis at the venue.

Time Limit

  • The allotted time for the arrangement is 2 hours.

Materials Provided

  • The organisers will provide only the display table.
  • Participants must bring all other materials, including a tablecloth, if required.

Reporting

  • All participants must report to the venue by 1:30 PM.

Judging Criteria

Arrangements will be judged on:

  • Creativity and Innovation
  • Visual Impact
  • Presentation and Neatness
  • Harmony of Colours and Design Elements

Final Decision

  • The decisions of the judges and organisers shall be final.
